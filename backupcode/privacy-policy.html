Legal & Security

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This policy outlines how BdDoc24 collects, uses, and protects your personal and medical information in compliance with applicable laws in Bangladesh.

1. Introduction

Welcome to BdDoc24. We operate a digital healthcare platform that connects patients with healthcare providers across Bangladesh. This Privacy Policy applies to the BdDoc24 website, mobile applications, and related services (collectively, the "Services"). By using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We are committed to ensuring the security and confidentiality of your personal and health-related data.

2. Information We Collect

To provide effective healthcare services, we collect specific types of information:

A Personal Identification Information

When you register or book an appointment, we collect your Full Name, Phone Number, Email Address, Gender, and Date of Birth. This is necessary to create your account and facilitate communication with the doctor.

B Health & Medical Information

If you choose to share it, we may collect information regarding your symptoms, medical history, previous prescriptions, and lab reports. This data is strictly used to help doctors provide better consultation and is shared only with the specific doctor you are consulting.

C Technical & Usage Data

We automatically collect IP addresses, browser type, device information, and usage patterns (e.g., pages visited) to analyze trends, administer the site, and track user movement to improve user experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

BdDoc24 uses the collected data for the following specific purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To process appointments, send booking confirmations, and enable telemedicine consultations.
  • Communication: To send appointment reminders, health tips, and updates regarding your consultation.
  • Improvement: To analyze usage data to fix bugs and develop new features.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with legal obligations or respond to lawful requests from public authorities.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We respect your privacy and do not sell your personal data. We share information only under these circumstances:

With Healthcare Providers

When you book an appointment, we share necessary details (Name, Phone, Reason for Visit) with the doctor or clinic to prepare for your consultation.

With Service Providers: We use third-party vendors (e.g., SMS gateways, payment processors) to perform services on our behalf. They are contractually bound to protect your data.

For Legal Requirements: We may disclose data if required by law or in response to valid legal requests from government authorities.

5. Data Security Measures

Protecting your sensitive health data is our highest priority. We implement robust security measures, including:

Encryption

All data transmissions are secured using 256-bit SSL encryption.

Access Control

Strict role-based access controls ensure only authorized personnel can view data.

Secure Storage

Data is stored in secure, audited servers with regular backups.

Audit Logs

We maintain logs of access and changes to sensitive data for accountability.

6. Your Rights

You have specific rights regarding your data held by BdDoc24:

  • Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: You can update your profile information or request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: You can request the deletion of your account and associated data, subject to legal retention requirements.

7. Cookies & Tracking

We use "cookies" and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our Service and hold certain information. Cookies are files with small amounts of data which may include an anonymous unique identifier. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent.

Questions about this Policy?

If you have any concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact our Data Protection Officer.
