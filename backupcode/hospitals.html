About Rajshahi City Hospital

Rajshahi City Hospital is a leading tertiary care hospital in the northern region of Bangladesh. Established with a vision to provide affordable yet world-class healthcare, we are equipped with the latest medical technology and a team of highly skilled professionals dedicated to patient well-being.

Diagnosis & Treatments

We provide comprehensive diagnosis and treatment for Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopedics, Gynecology & Obstetrics, General Medicine, Pediatrics, Gastroenterology, and Dermatology. Our emergency unit is equipped to handle trauma and critical care cases 24/7.

Other Facilities

  • 24/7 Emergency & Trauma Care
  • Modern ICU & CCU
  • Advanced Operation Theaters (OT)
  • NICU & PICU for Children
  • Digital X-Ray, CT Scan & MRI
  • In-house Pharmacy & Pathology Lab
