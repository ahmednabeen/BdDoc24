Trust & Safety

Doctor Verification Policy

Ensuring patient safety is the cornerstone of BdDoc24. We employ a strict, multi-layered verification protocol to validate the credentials of every healthcare provider on our platform.

1. Our Mission: Zero Tolerance for Quackery

In Bangladesh, the proliferation of unlicensed medical practitioners poses a severe risk to public health. BdDoc24 is committed to being a safe harbor where patients can find doctors with absolute confidence. We adhere to the guidelines set forth by the Bangladesh Medical and Dental Council (BMDC). Our policy is designed to filter out unqualified practitioners and ensure that every profile on our platform represents a legitimate, licensed medical professional.

2. The 4-Step Verification Process

1

Primary Source Verification (BMDC)

Every doctor must provide their BMDC Registration Number. We cross-reference this number with the official BMDC database (or physical registry) to confirm:

  • The registration number is valid and active.
  • The name on the profile matches the registered name exactly.
  • The registration category (General/Specialist) matches the claimed specialty.
2

Educational & Qualification Review

We require high-resolution scans of all claimed degrees. Our verification team checks for:

Basic Qualification

MBBS/BDS certificates from recognized medical colleges listed by the Ministry of Health.

Post-Graduation

FCPS, MD, MS, or Diploma certificates verified against the issuing university/institution (e.g., BSMMU, BCPS).

3

Identity & Biometric Validation

To prevent identity theft, we require a clear scan of the doctor's National ID (NID) or Passport. Furthermore, we utilize a live selfie check during the onboarding process to ensure the person submitting the documents is the same individual appearing on the ID cards.

Final Approval & Badge Issuance

Upon successful completion of all checks, the profile is marked as "Verified" with a green shield badge. This badge is visible to patients and signifies that the doctor has passed our rigorous vetting process.

3. Document Requirements

To ensure a smooth verification process, uploaded documents must meet the following criteria:

Document Type Requirements
BMDC Certificate Must be valid and not expired. Clear scan of both sides if applicable.
Degree Certificates High-resolution scan (no camera flash glare). Must show the doctor's name, passing year, and university seal.
NID / Passport Must match the name on the BMDC certificate. NID must be a double-sided scan.
Profile Photo Recent passport-sized photo against a white background, or a selfie taken within the app.

Verification Timeline

Standard verification takes 3 to 5 business days. Incomplete documents or discrepancies with BMDC records may delay the process. Doctors can track their status via the "My Profile" dashboard.

4. Continuous Monitoring & Re-verification

Verification is not a one-time event. BdDoc24 performs periodic audits to ensure compliance:

  • Annual Renewal: Doctors are required to update their BMDC renewal status annually.
  • Disciplinary Action: We monitor public records for any suspensions or revocations. If a doctor's license is suspended, their profile is immediately deactivated.
  • Patient Feedback: Patterns of malpractice complaints reported by patients trigger an immediate internal review.

Report a Concern

If you suspect a doctor is unlicensed or their credentials are falsified, please report it immediately. We treat all reports with strict confidentiality.
